If you're new to cycling, here are some essential tips to get you started on the right path.

## Choose the Right Bike

Selecting the right bike for your needs is crucial:

- **Road bikes** are great for speed and paved surfaces
- **Mountain bikes** excel on rough terrain and trails
- **Hybrid bikes** offer versatility for multiple surfaces

## Safety First

Always prioritize safety when cycling:

1. Wear a properly fitted helmet
2. Use lights and reflectors for visibility
3. Follow traffic rules and signals
4. Wear bright or reflective clothing

## Maintain Your Bike

Regular maintenance keeps your bike running smoothly:

- Check tire pressure before each ride
- Keep your chain clean and lubricated
- Inspect brakes regularly
- Schedule professional tune-ups

Happy cycling!
