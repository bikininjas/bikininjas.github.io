Proper maintenance is essential to keep your bike performing at its best throughout the year.

## Spring Maintenance

As the weather warms up:

- Clean off winter grime and salt
- Check for rust and corrosion
- Lubricate the chain and moving parts
- Inspect tires for winter damage

## Summer Care

During heavy riding season:

- Clean your bike regularly
- Check tire pressure weekly
- Keep the drivetrain clean and lubricated
- Inspect brake pads for wear

## Fall Preparation

Before the weather turns:

- Apply rust inhibitor to vulnerable parts
- Check lights and reflectors
- Tighten all bolts and fittings
- Consider fenders for wet conditions

## Winter Storage

If storing your bike:

- Clean thoroughly before storage
- Store in a dry location
- Consider a bike cover
- Inflate tires to proper pressure

Regular maintenance extends the life of your bike and ensures safe riding conditions year-round.
